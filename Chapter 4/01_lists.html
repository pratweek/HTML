Steps to bake a cake

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Mix the dry ingredients in a bowl.
  3. Add wet ingredients and stir until smooth.
  4. Pour the batter into a greased cake pan.
  5. Bake for 30-35 minutes or until a toothpick comes out clean.
  6. Let the cake cool before frosting.

Ingredients for the cake

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 ½ cups sugar
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt
